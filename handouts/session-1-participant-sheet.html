Session 1 · Decision support · 2 / 2

The Google consent screen: a worked example

Illustrative — rehearse with the facilitator. You are signing into your own dedicated practice Google account, containing only made-up files. Check the email shown on the Google screen; it must be your practice account, not a customer, bank or personal-data account.

  • Expected access: full Drive access for this practice account. This is broad access, not a fence around the training folder. The instruction to use one folder does not restrict Google’s permission.
  • Expected file: the facilitator’s Google sign-in setup file (the “client file”) lives in your own computer’s settings folder, outside the Financial App project: ~/.config/google-drive-mcp/gcp-oauth.keys.json, or the matching location for your computer. Let the facilitator locate it; do not paste its contents into chat.
  • You do the sign-in: use your own browser. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, tokens or callback links to the agent.
  • Anything else: stop and ask. Wrong account, unexpected access, blocked sign-in, or an unfamiliar setup goes to the facilitator. Sign-in lasts about a week while Google’s setup is in test mode.

Before you start

Confirm with bank IT before class that this Drive tool may be used. Use the exact facilitator-chosen version, not an automatic upgrade. The permission-setting step is untested too: if OpenCode does not ask, stop before a Drive read.

Keep the boundaries clear

The prompt asks. Settings enforce. File contents are data, not instructions. Do not approve overwriting, deleting, moving or changing sharing for this exercise.

Practice only. Never treat this exercise as bank-approved security or investment advice.
