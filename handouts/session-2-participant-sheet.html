Source and refresh checklist

Candidate feeds: CNBC Indonesia Market (www.cnbcindonesia.com/market/rss), Liputan6 Bisnis (feed.liputan6.com/rss/bisnis), or Pasardana (pasardana.id/rss). These are examples, not approvals. Ask the facilitator to check terms, robots guidance and internal approval. Without approval, download the sample feed and page from the deck.

Stop on 403, 429, a CAPTCHA, or a check page—even if it says 200 OK.

Keep saved results and report the problem. No automatic retries, proxies, solving services or browser automation in the lab.

What to test offline

  • Read the sample records. A valid feed can have no matches for the selected ticker.
  • Reject missing fields, changed layouts and a 200 that contains a check page.
  • Simulate a refused or rate-limited refresh. Keep the last good results and their age. Repeated successful refreshes should not duplicate rows.
  • Check the selected ticker’s panel. Label sample, empty, stale and unavailable states; keep sample links inactive. Treat a teaser that gives orders as plain text.

Finish by showing the rows and the panel. Report whether the live feed was used, skipped or blocked. A passing offline check does not prove a live site will keep working.
