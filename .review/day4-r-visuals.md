# Day 4 decks — visuals and slide craft review

Checked: all 52 slide PNGs and all 21 prompt-panel PNGs in `.review/day4-shots/`, each looked at in 2×2 contact sheets (each image shrunk to 900 px wide, so text sizes below are estimates). Also read: the visible word counts in `.review/day4-text/` and the title and word rules in `AGENTS.md`. Not checked: the decks in a browser (no screenshot was unclear enough to need it), print layout, the speaker notes beyond the word counts, and the lower part of long prompts (a panel PNG shows only the top). In the s1 and s2 panel PNGs the page behind the panel is always slide 18 (s3: slide 16), so the panel is not tied to the slide that opens it; this is a capture effect, not a finding.

Facts recorded once, not findings:
- Session 2's titles are short single sentences, mostly one line. This is the owner decision of 2026-11-17. Session 1's titles are longer and often wrap to two lines.
- Titles: every title in all three decks is a full-sentence statement. No topic labels, no question titles.
- Word budget: no slide is over budget. Visible word counts are s1 20–54, s2 23–57, s3 15–25. The two highest, s1 slide 17 (54) and s2 slide 17 (57), are exercise slides (limit 60). Several s2 slides (7, 8, 9, 14, 18) and s1 slide 3 sit at exactly 40, the limit for presented slides.

## Verdict per deck
- s1: Clean, consistent card-and-arrow look; most diagrams read left to right and match their titles. Weak points are a quiz slide that gives away its answer, a few very small labels (rotated "APP / AGENT", terminal mock, disclaimers) and slide 15, where the order of the steps is not shown.
- s2: The strongest deck visually. Diagrams are simple (two columns, one row, one table), arrows are labelled and the audit-trail pictures teach well. Issues are small: one more quiz that gives its answer in the title, small text inside the mock-directory card, and an end slide that refers to "steps 1 to 3" that were never numbered.
- s3: Reads as a sibling, not the same course. It uses a different drawing style (sketchy line art, grey-green dashed arrows, no cards), tighter title letter-spacing, no left footer and no opening hero slide. Several diagrams have arrows running through labels or loose elements with unclear meaning (slides 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 13, 14). Its prompts are very long walls of text.

## What works well
- s2 slide 10: a clear left-to-right picture with labelled results (200 and 403) and two people tagged with their roles. Plain, large and well matched to the title.
- s2 slides 12 and 16: the "who, what, when" cards joined to the dark log row, then the real log table, teach the audit trail in order.
- s1 slides 4 and 8: simple three-card rows with solid red arrows and large labels. s1 slide 9 uses colour dots (green, amber, red) well for fresh, cached and unavailable.
- s1 and s2 slides 17: the practice timeline is clear, with times in red, an icon and a short line for each block, and the prompt buttons at the foot.
- s3 slide 15: the five-step time line with big circles and times reads well, and s3 slide 16 ends with a picture that matches its title.
