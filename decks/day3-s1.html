LLM basics

An LLM predicts the next words from the text you send it

Text in:"BBCA is the ticker of Bank Central ..."
Text out:"Asia"
Large language model = LLM
LLM basics

The model is stateless: it remembers nothing between requests

Request 1:

"My name is Sari."

Reply:

"Nice to meet you, Sari."

Request 2, sent alone:

"What is my name?"

Reply:

"I don't know."

Model selection

Fast models answer in seconds; smart models reason before they act

Fast model:

quick answers, good for small jobs

Smart model:

slower, thinks first, good for hard jobs

In opencode: type /models to pick one.
Model selection

Use fast models for small edits and smart models for planning and debugging

Fast:
rename a title, fix a typo, change a colour
Smart:
write the plan, find why something breaks
Wrong choice costs time, or quality.
